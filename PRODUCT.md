# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Bilal is the primary user. He uses the dashboard throughout the workday to run his independent web business: monitoring the portfolio website, qualifying leads, managing active client work, sending proposals and invoices, and maintaining public content.

## Product Purpose

The dashboard brings website performance and client operations into one responsive control center. Success means Bilal can understand what needs attention, see how the site is performing, and move work forward without switching between disconnected tools.

## Positioning

One calm workspace for running the website and the client business behind it.

## Brand Personality

Modern, comprehensive, and clear. The product should feel capable and information-rich without becoming crowded or visually noisy.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy crypto-dashboard styling, decorative charts, excessive gradients, nested cards, and dense enterprise interfaces that hide the next action.

## Design Principles

- Put decisions before decoration.
- Show the whole business while keeping the next action obvious.
- Use familiar controls and consistent states so the interface disappears into the work.
- Make every data view useful on both desktop and mobile.
- Distinguish sample, delayed, and live data honestly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with keyboard-operable controls, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, semantic landmarks, reduced-motion support, and touch targets of at least 44 pixels on compact screens.
